# Tracks are drafted by an LLM into files, then reviewed

The Learner states what they want to build, an LLM drafts the whole Track as files, and the Learner reviews and edits those files before following them. We rejected hand-writing every Track (too slow, and it defeats learning topics you don't know yet) and generating Steps live during a session (non-repeatable, can't be corrected, can't be shared). Files on disk keep Tracks stable, fixable, and shareable once the project is open-sourced.
